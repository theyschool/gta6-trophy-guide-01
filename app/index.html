Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

1 Story 60–80 h

Play the five chapters; take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals on the way — both count toward 100% later. Keep a save at each chapter's start and end: no mission replay.

12 story trophies · 1 gold at the end

2 100% Completion ~100 h

After the credits: Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Lenient — about 60% true completion: 200/277 vehicles, 100/255 animals, 300 stunts, 70/80 challenges, 100 optional missions, 8 bank vaults, 39 gang operations, 15 target decks, 15/18 meditations. Nothing missable.

A Long Road (gold) · most silver & bronze

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and follow her to the balcony to learn to meditate. That home meditation is gone after Chapter 1. Everything else can be earned after the story. Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap

No online trophies. All 55 GTA VI trophies are single-player and offline — there is nothing to earn in an online mode, and the platinum can never expire with a server. (PowerPyx; GTA Zone.)
