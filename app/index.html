    Road to the platinum

    About 200 hours at difficulty 4 out of 10 (PowerPyx). One playthrough is enough, and there are no online trophies. The order below is built from the official descriptions.

      Missable: just one

      Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that may be missable. Meditate at all 18 spots — and do the first one, Lucia’s balcony meditation at home in Chapter 1, before that chapter ends. It becomes permanently unavailable after Chapter 1, and there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

      Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase

      Not yet confirmed

      Eight trophies are marked Unconfirmed because no guide has pinned down their trigger yet. This guide says so plainly instead of guessing — check PowerPyx or GTABase for the latest before you chase them.
